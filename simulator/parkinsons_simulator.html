Parkinson’s Disease Digital Twin (PD-BCR): 90-Day Cellular Energy & Staged Recovery Simulator

Plain-English Overview: Dopamine-producing neurons in the brain's Substantia Nigra (SNc) fire continuously like cardiac pacemakers (3.42× higher energy demand). In Parkinson’s disease, these neurons get trapped by (1) clogged waste disposal (lysosomes), (2) damaged mitochondrial power plants, and (3) unused oxygen buildup (hyperoxia) that oxidizes dopamine. Taking energy boosters (NAD⁺ or Creatine) on Day 1 backfires—copying damaged mitochondrial DNA faster (The Simultaneous Biogenesis Trap). This simulator models a 3-Stage Chronological Reset over 90 days: Stage 0 (d1–14: Unclog Waste & Clamp O₂) → Stage 1 (d15–34: Recycle Damaged Mitochondria) → Stage 2 (d35–90: Multiply Healthy Mitochondria & De-Age DNA).

1. Why a 3-Stage Chronological Reset (`PD-BCR`)?

Most Parkinson's supplements and clinical trials test a single drug or give everything at once. Our 10-state differential equation engine proves that order matters:
• Stage 0 (Days 1–14): Unclogs lysosomal waste doors with Ambroxol (PDB: 2NSX) and shields brain tissue from excess unused oxygen (GBT601 + PT2399).
• Stage 1 (Days 15–34): Tags and recycles damaged mitochondria (D) using Kinetin + Urolithin A (PDB: 6EQI) while holding NAD⁺/Creatine off so damaged DNA isn't cloned.
• Stage 2 (Days 35–90): Once blood GDF15 confirms damaged mitochondria are below 8%, pulses NR + Creatine + Folate/B2 + α-KG to rebuild clean mitochondria and reverse cellular epigenetic age (Berg et al. 2026).
