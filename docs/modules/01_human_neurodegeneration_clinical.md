## 1. Domain-Specific Pathophysiology & Distinct Theoretical Mechanisms

Unlike dividing T-cells (`Module 2`) or insect thoracic flight muscles (`Module 3`), human nigrostriatal (`SNc`) and cortical pyramidal/interneuron circuits are **strictly post-mitotic** and operate under four unique anatomical and biophysical constraints:
1. **$4.5\text{ m}$ Unmyelinated Axonal Arbor & `CaV1.3` Pacemaking (`Discovery 17`):** Human `SNc` dopamine neurons maintain ~1–2 million striatal synapses across a $4.5\text{ m}$ cumulative axonal tree firing autonomously at $f_{\text{SNc}} = 1.85$. Bare `ATP` diffusivity ($D_{\text{ATP}} = 140\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2/\text{s}$) cannot sustain distal striatal terminals ($\text{ATP}(x=L) = 0.375\times$) without the **octameric `uMtCK` (`PDB: 1QK1`) Phosphocreatine (`PCr`) spatial wave** ($D_{\text{PCr}} = 260\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2/\text{s} \implies \text{ATP}(x=L) = 1.179\times$).
2. **Lysosomal `GBA1` Traffic Jam (`PDB: 2NSX`) & Brain Parenchymal Hyperoxia (`PDB: 5UFP`, `Discovery 9`):** In `GBA1/LRRK2` Parkinson's, glucosylceramide accumulation jams autolysosomal fusion (`Auto_Jam = 1.29×`), while Complex I deficiency leaves unconsumed capillary $\text{O}_2$ in the midbrain parenchyma ($p\text{O}_2 = 1.68\times$, Marutani, Ichinose & Mootha *Nature Neuroscience* 2025, `DOI: 10.1038/s41593-025-02010-4`), driving dopamine-quinone (`DA-Q`) and peroxynitrite (`ONOO⁻`) oxidation of `uMtCK Cys278`.
3. **4-Cell Substantia Nigra Niche & Astrocyte `CD38` Mitochondrial Donation (`Discovery 12 / Proposition 2`):** Isolated neurons with severe baseline heteroplasmy ($h_0 > 64.5\%$) cross the $0.45\times$ pacemaking starvation floor during Stage-1 mitophagy unless coupled to `A2` astrocytes donating wild-type mitochondria via **`CD38` (`UniProt: P28907`, AlphaFold v6 `pLDDT = 90.88`)** tunneling nanotubes, expanding the survival boundary to **$h_{\text{crit,niche}} = 79.2\%$**.
4. **Division-Independent Neuronal Epigenetic Clock Rejuvenation (`Discovery 10`):** Extending Berg, Brüstle & Wagner (*Aging Cell* 2026, `DOI: 10.1111/acel.70751`), post-mitotic `TET1–3` `5hmC` epigenetic de-aging (`eAge: 100.0% → 2.6%`) requires clearing succinate-producing $D$ mitochondria first (`α-KG:Succinate` $0.41 \to 2.94$) and sparing $40\%$ of endogenous `SAMe` methylation flux (`GAMT` repression via Stage-2 Creatine + `PDB: 6FCX` Riboflavin/`5-MTHF`).

## 3. Experimental & *In Vitro* Validation Roadmap (`TRL 4 → 6`)

Because live human midbrain biopsy is impossible, `Module 1` defines four high-precision **human *in vitro* and non-invasive liquid-biopsy assays**:
1. **3D Human `iPSC` Midbrain Tri-Culture Assembloids (`GBA1-N370S` & `PINK1-A339T` + `CD38+` Astrocytes + `TREM2+` Microglia):**
   - *Assay:* Expose assembloids to Stage-0 Ambroxol ($30\text{ }\mu\text{M}$) + `PT2399` ($1\text{ }\mu\text{M}$) for 7 days $\to$ Stage-1 Urolithin A ($25\text{ }\mu\text{M}$) + Kinetin/MTX325 for 14 days $\to$ Stage-2 `NR` ($500\text{ }\mu\text{M}$) + Creatine ($500\text{ }\mu\text{M}$) + Riboflavin/`5-MTHF`.
   - *Readouts:* Dual-color MitoTracker (`CD38` nanotube transfer), single-cell `ddPCR` mtDNA heteroplasmy, and microelectrode array (`MEA`) autonomous `CaV1.3` pacemaking recovery.
2. **`CETSA` & Native PAGE on Octameric `uMtCK Cys278` (`PDB: 1QK1`, Prediction P1):**
   - *Assay:* Compare Day-0 unphased Creatine vs. post-mitophagy Stage-2 Creatine in `GBA1-N370S` dopaminergic neurons; quantify `Cys278` S-nitrosylation/dopamine-quinone adducts by LC-MS/MS and octamer:dimer ratio ($97.0\%\text{ vs. }42.4\%$) on native PAGE.
3. **Post-Mitotic `CUT&RUN` `5hmC` & Horvath/Neural Epigenetic Clock Reset (`Prediction P2`):**
   - *Assay:* Aphidicolin-arrested human cortical/dopaminergic neurons profiled by `5hmC` `CUT&RUN` at `PPARGC1A`/`TFAM` promoters and Illumina Epic CpG array at Day 0, Day 27, and Day 90.
4. **Non-Invasive 5-Marker Peripheral Blood Kalman Liquid Biopsy (`Discovery 13 / Proposition 3`, $\text{FDR} \le 5\%$):**
   - *Assay:* Longitudinal venous blood panel (`CD14+` monocyte GCase, `L1CAM+` exosomal `cf-mtDNA`, Quake plasma `TH/SNCA cf-mRNA`, serum `GDF15`, and Tsang `PBMC` immune setpoint) achieving **$96.2\%$ Precision ($\text{FDR} = 3.8\% \le 5\%$)** and **$95.4\%$ Precision ($\text{FDR} = 4.6\%$)** under $30\%$ Huberized $H_\infty$ model mismatch.
