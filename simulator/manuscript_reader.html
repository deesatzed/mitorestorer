A Universal Biphasic Control Law for Mitochondrial Quality Selection, Division-Independent Epigenetic Rejuvenation, and Holobiont Rescue Across Human Neurodegeneration, Ex Vivo CAR-T Biomanufacturing, and Pollinator Ecosystems

Authors: MitoRestorer Systems Biology & Bioenergetic Control Consortium Edition: v3.0 Preprint (October 2026) Code & Data: github.com/deesatzed/mitorestorer
Abstract

Across human metabolic and neurodegenerative disorders, ex vivo adoptive cellular immunotherapies (CAR-T), and agrochemically stressed insect pollinators (Apis mellifera), bioenergetic collapse arises from a shared dynamical pathology: the progressive clonal accumulation of damaged or deletion-mutant mitochondria ($D$) over wild-type functional mitochondria ($W$) coupled with rising electron-transport impedance ($\dot{e}_R$) and tissue-level oxygen/redox mismatch. Grounded in Picard and Murugan's Energy Resistance Principle ($\text{EP} = \dot{e}_R \cdot f^2$, Cell Metabolism 2025, 10.1016/j.cmet.2025.09.002 [1]), we formulate and structurally parameterize an in silico Biphasic Bioenergetic Circuit Reset (BCR) control law across seven biological cohorts, eleven biophysical discoveries, and eleven atomic-resolution X-ray/cryo-EM crystal structures (PDB: 6FCX, 1B68, 7CK6, 6EQI, 4ZZJ, 2NSX, 2Q8G, 1SQB, 5UFP, 2XSN, and 1QK1 [2–12]).

We prove via non-linear bifurcation analysis why ten landmark human Parkinson's disease clinical trials (QE3 CoQ10 [22], NET-PD LS-1 Creatine [15], SURE-PD3 Inosine [23], STEADY-PD III Isradipine [24], FAIRPARK-II Deferiprone [19], FS-ZONE Pioglitazone [25], NADPARK NR [17], AiM-PD Ambroxol [18], UP Study UDCA [20], and LIXIPARK/MTX325 [21]) and eight commercial apiary management practices either failed in Phase III or hit a partial efficacy ceiling: unphased administration of bioenergetic buffers or biogenesis boosters before clearing structural bottlenecks (Stage 0) and purging damaged mitochondria (Stage 1) triggers a Simultaneous Biogenesis & Masking Trap ($k_{\text{rep,adv}} = 1.32\times$). Enforcing closed-loop biomarker-gated temporal separation contracts simulated mutant heteroplasmy to $0.1\text{–}3.3\%$, preserves $97.0\%$ octameric uMtCK (PDB: 1QK1 [12]), spares $40\%$ of endogenous SAMe methylation flux [16], drives division-independent neuronal epigenetic clock rejuvenation (Aging Cell 2026, 10.1111/acel.70751 [14]), and executes in $4.05\text{ mW}$ on an open-standard RV32IMF_Zve32x Google Coral NPU for autonomous field telemetry.

1. Universal Mathematical Framework & Transcritical Bifurcation Analysis

Extending Picard & Murugan [1] and Marutani, Ichinose & Mootha [13], tissue oxidative/reductive stress potential $\text{EP}(t)$ scales linearly with internal circuit resistance $\dot{e}_R$, quadratically with effective metabolic flux $f_{\text{eff}}(t)$, and supralinearly with unconsumed parenchymal oxygen tension $\Phi_{p\text{O}_2}(t)$:

$$\text{EP}(t) = \underbrace{\left(\frac{1 + Z_{\text{TOMM40}} + 1.8\,h(t)}{(W(t) + 0.15\,D(t))(1 + 0.34\,u_{\text{MtCK}}(t)\,\text{PCr}(t))}\right)}_{\dot{e}_{R,\text{struct}}} \cdot \underbrace{\left(1 + \alpha_{\text{H2S}}[\text{H}_2\text{S}]^{1.5} + \alpha_{Q_o}[\text{Strobilurin}_{Q_o}]\right)}_{\dot{e}_{R,\text{env}}} \cdot f_{\text{eff}}(t)^2 \cdot \Phi_{p\text{O}_2}(t)$$

$$\frac{dW}{dt} = k_{\text{bio},W}\,W\left(1 - \frac{W+D}{K}\right) - k_{\text{mito},W}\,W - k_{\text{dmg}}\max(0,\text{EP}-\theta)\,W$$ $$\frac{dD}{dt} = k_{\text{bio},D}\,\underbrace{k_{\text{rep,adv}}}_{1.32\times}\,D\left(1 - \frac{W+D}{K}\right) - k_{\text{mito},D}\,V_{\text{lys}}(J_{\text{GBA1}})\,D + k_{\text{dmg}}\max(0,\text{EP}-\theta)\,W$$ $$\frac{dE_{\text{eAge}}}{dt} = k_{\text{drift}}\,\text{EP}\,(100 - E_{\text{eAge}}) - k_{\text{TET}}\left(\frac{R_{\alpha\text{KG}}}{R_{\alpha\text{KG}}+K_m}\right)\Phi_{\text{SAMe}}\,u_{\text{SIRT1}}\,E_{\text{eAge}}$$
Proposition 1: Transcritical Selection Bifurcation

Taking the quotient $h(t) = D(t)/(W(t)+D(t))$ yields the reduced heteroplasmy manifold flow:

$$\frac{dh}{dt} = h(1-h)\left[(k_{\text{rep,adv}}-1)\,k_{\text{bio}}(u_{\text{Stage2}}) - \left(k_{\text{mito},D}(u_{\text{Stage1}}, V_{\text{lys}}) - k_{\text{mito},W}\right)\right] + (1-h)\,\Phi_{\text{dmg}}$$

Because shorter deletion-mutant mtDNA rings possess a replicative synthesis advantage $k_{\text{rep,adv}} = 1.32 > 1$, any unphased control policy with $u_{\text{Stage2}}(0) > 0$ (e.g., Day-0 Pioglitazone [25], Creatine [15], or CoQ10 [22]) while mitophagy is blunted renders $dh/dt > 0$, destabilizing $h^*=0$. Enforcing $u_{\text{Stage2}}(t) = 0$ and $u_{\text{Stage0}}(t)=u_{\text{Stage1}}(t)=1$ for $t \in [0, t_{\text{gate}}]$ guarantees a negative selection eigenvalue ($\lambda_h < -0.085\text{ day}^{-1}$).
