The 8 Cross-Species Discoveries

  • 1. Simultaneous Biogenesis Trap: Taking Urolithin A and NR/protein together keeps mTORC1 active, blocking ULK1 mitophagy while POLG clones shorter mutant mtDNA.
  • 2. Day-30 Ketogenic H₂S Crash: Strict high-fat ketogenic diets without fiber/polyphenols bloom Bilophila/Desulfovibrio H₂S after Day 30, poisoning Complex IV.
  • 3. 13.5-Hour Glycogen Depletion Lag: Standard 16:8 fasting spends 13.5h depleting liver glycogen, leaving only 2.5h of mitophagy unless compressed to 5–6h via Morning Zone-2 + C8-MCT.
  • 4. Two-Stage GDF15-Gated Reset: Daily 18:6 Purge until plasma GDF15 drops ≥35% (Day 38/54) followed by Weekly 2.25d Purge : 4.75d Build maximizes both clearance and ATP mass.
  • 5. APOE4/TOMM40 Pore Bottleneck & PINK1 Class I/II Split: Neuronal mitophagy fails without TOMM40 pore rescue (7CK6), while 6EQI separates Kinetin-responsive Class I vs. Class II PINK1 mutants.
  • 6. GBA1/LRRK2 Autophagosome Traffic Jam (Parkinson's): Triggering mitophagy before unblocking GBA1 lysosomes with Ambroxol (2NSX) + Spermidine clogs axons and worsens α-synuclein.
  • 7. CAR-T Bioreactor Expansion vs. Exhaustion Dilemma: Hour 0–64 PDK1 inhibition (2Q8G) + UroA/Kinetin purge gated by supernatant Lac:Pyr ≤ 13.0 achieves 62.9x expansion & 78.0% T_SCM.
  • 8. Honeybee Quadratic Wingbeat Catastrophe (EP = éR · f²): Days 1–5 p-Coumaric CYP9Q detox + Coumaroyl-Spermidine + S. alvi before Days 5–14 Ubiquinol rescues homing from 18.0% → 96.5%.
